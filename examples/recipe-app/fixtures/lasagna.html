Classic Lasagna

This is the best lasagna & it feeds a crowd.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 3 cups mozzarella, shredded

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Layer noodles, sauce and cheese.
  3. Bake for 45 minutes.
